# AI General Assembly Training Programme

Welcome to the AI General Assembly Training Programme at Thomas Adewumi University, Kwara, Nigeria. This comprehensive curriculum is designed to equip final-year students with essential 21st-century skills in data science, machine learning, business analytics and digital entrepreneurship through the practical application of cutting-edge AI tools. You will enhance your productivity, foster creativity and prepare for the dynamic job market of tomorrow.

For more details on course schedules, instructors and resources, please visit our programme website:
<https://tauniversity.github.io/cohort2025webpage/>

## Course Overview

The programme is structured into seven main modules, each focusing on a different toolset:

1. **Data Analytics using Excel**
   Unlock the power of spreadsheets by mastering advanced formulas, pivot tables and pivot charts, data cleaning with Power Query and dynamic dashboard design.

2. **Data Analytics using R**
   Harness statistical computing with R by learning data manipulation with dplyr and tidyr, statistical modelling, advanced visualisations with ggplot2 and reproducible research practices using Quarto.

3. **Machine Learning with Python**
   Build intelligent systems in Python by understanding key machine learning algorithms, data preprocessing and feature engineering, model evaluation and deployment with scikit-learn, TensorFlow and Keras.

4. **Power BI**
   Transform data into actionable insights by connecting to diverse data sources, designing interactive reports and dashboards, managing data models and collaborating via the Power BI service.

5. **Mastering Educational AI Tools**
   Unlock AI power with platforms such as ChatGPT, NotebookLM, Gemini and Gamma to automate tasks, streamline workflows, generate creative content and apply AI ethically across finance, healthcare and business operations.

6. **LinkedIn Optimisation**
   Enhance your professional presence by crafting a compelling LinkedIn profile, showcasing skills and achievements, growing and engaging your network and leveraging platform features for job searching and personal branding.

7. **Digital Marketing and Business Venture**
   Launch and grow a digital enterprise by mastering SEO and SEM, social media marketing and content creation, email marketing analytics and performance measurement, and end-to-end digital business management.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this programme, you will be able to:

- Analyse and visualise data effectively using Excel, R and Power BI.
- Perform advanced statistical analyses and produce reproducible reports with R and Quarto.
- Develop, evaluate and deploy machine learning models using Python libraries.
- Integrate AI tools to automate workflows, generate content and solve real-world problems responsibly.
- Optimise your LinkedIn profile to attract opportunities and expand your professional network.
- Design and implement data-driven digital marketing campaigns and launch successful online ventures.
